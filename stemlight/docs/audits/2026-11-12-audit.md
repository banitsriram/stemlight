## Bottom line

The audio and input foundations are strong and tested: one clock, sample-exact loops, a clean gesture engine, saving. On the judged lines that matters little yet. Everything a judge can see or touch is missing: creatures, remake surfaces, tutorial, end, sound effects, video. Today the experience is notes drifting out of empty black space while music plays, with gestures on invisible targets. Three of the four criteria are carried almost entirely by B's open tasks, so the fastest score gain is B's creature and remake-surface work (proposals 1–2), while A takes the small cold-start and copy fixes and the listening pass.
